Our Journey

Our History

From a small language tutoring centre to one of the region's most trusted English institutions.

2009

The Beginning

Founded by a team of passionate English educators with just 3 classrooms and 50 students. Our mission: make high-quality English education accessible to all.

2014

Rapid Growth

Expanded to 500+ students and introduced online learning. Partnered with Cambridge Assessment for internationally accredited certification programmes.

2019

Going Digital

Launched our fully interactive e-learning platform, enabling students from 20+ countries to learn English remotely with live virtual classes.

2024

Today & Beyond

With over 2,500 active students, 50+ certified teachers, and a 98% satisfaction rate, we continue to innovate and set the standard for English education excellence.
